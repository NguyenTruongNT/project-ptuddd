# OAuth button assets

- `google_g.png`: unmodified Google G icon from
  https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png.
  Usage: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
- `github_mark.png`: unmodified `GitHub_Invertocat_Black.png` from the official
  https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip download.
  Usage: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo.
- The button font is the unmodified Android static `GoogleSans-Medium.ttf`
  from Google Sans v14.000, published at
  https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/releases/tag/v14.000.
  Font license: `../fonts/OFL.txt` (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Logos identify the authentication providers. They do not indicate endorsement
of StudyShare by Google or GitHub.
